## Decision and next experiment

The tenant-wide retained permit budget is default8, configurable1..32 and includes inactive/expired rows. Its128-event history reserves the terminal revocation slot. Registration still writes six actual authority rows. Only the trusted isolated membership-withdrawal method invalidates permits atomically; existing session, membership and direct-state writers were not rewired. This is insufficient for a production permit protocol, even where that narrow control passes.

Keep the current real fences. A bounded next candidate can investigate shortening the fenced critical section through immutable-evidence validation, with current source-head/CAS and all authority checks repeated inside the final transaction. It first needs deterministic TOCTOU, revoke/replay and rollback proof; no safe reduction or cache/version-only revocation guarantee is assumed. A permit successor instead needs a complete, bounded, atomic authority-writer protocol and a preserved fault/error contract before integration review.

No F5-B adoption is authorized by this rejected experiment. A later accepted design still needs a matched current-parent before/after benchmark against unchanged targets. Historical54a7 results remain historical, including complete C8 successes, higher-concurrency contention and incomplete cells. HTTP latency, admission queue and total response remain separate. No benchmark was rerun here.
