# Frontend owner handoff

The frontend owner's maintained report is [UI-HANDOFF.md](../UI-HANDOFF.md). It records owned files, contracts, commands, test counts, external evidence and the failed browser attempts with their dispositions. [UX-REVIEW.md](../UX-REVIEW.md) maps before/after screenshots and remaining visual gates.

These are owner reports for independent lead review. They do not replace the final full-browser, clean-checkout or remote-head acceptance recorded in [LEAD-ACCEPTANCE.md](../LEAD-ACCEPTANCE.md).
