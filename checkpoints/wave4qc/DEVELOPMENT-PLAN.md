# Catalog stock continuation

Baseline: accepted catalog procurement and quarantine receiving `ae0602a`. This increment implements controlled inspection, approval and qualified putaway for real typed receipts. The frozen contract is [CONTRACT.md](CONTRACT.md). Final maintained-suite and connected-browser acceptance belongs to the lead-owned STATUS.md and LEAD-ACCEPTANCE.md; pending or absent results must not be reported as passed.

An immutable LOT quantity partition or exhaustive SERIAL identity partition can be recorded as inspection evidence. A separate approval action permanently binds the current inspection revision. Accepted quantity alone can be put away once, in full, into a current qualified company location with its actual bin authority. Rejected quantity and identities remain terminal quarantine within this scope. No supplier return, replacement, correction, rework, reopening or reversal of PO received entitlement is authorized. The maximum is 100 inspection revisions; revision 100 remains approvable, while a fresh revision 101 is refused.

Dedicated catalog stock balances, serial units and putaway movements preserve accepted/rejected conservation. Warehouse available/reserved counters remain zero. Physical typed available quantity is the stored accepted amount; live eligible quantity becomes zero under a receipt hold, source master revision/deactivation, location withdrawal or bin drift. Those changes preserve physical quantities and immutable evidence. A positive eligible quantity remains a read of current storage eligibility: allocation and execution are not implemented, and canAllocate/canExecute remain false.

QC read, record, approval and putaway use dedicated capabilities. Separate record/approval grants are not a two-person approval guarantee; an owner can perform both. A standalone qc.read user receives the actual source identities needed for inspection without gaining procurement or stock mutation access. Legacy receipt operations remain denied before and after typed storage, even when presentation markers are stripped. No current source may be relabeled as legacy to access quantity-only allocation, custody, manufacturing or shipment.

The next implementation boundary is a new controlled internal material-demand and execution contract. Freeze exact approved internal order/engineering demand, legal-entity ownership, source item revision/UOM/policy, current storage location, quantity/identity availability, holds and all write fences before adding a reservation. Model LOT and SERIAL per material line; the existing customer WO all-serial flag does not implement a mixed BOM. Approved demand must precede execution release so reservation-before-release does not create a circular prerequisite.

Then implement actual typed reservation, release/abandon where authorized, and issue exactly once. LOT reservation must conserve exact decimals and source lot/heat evidence; SERIAL reservation must bind the actual accepted AVAILABLE identities and their global registry. Both need common receipt/balance/unit/location/master CAS that competes with holds and every future stock writer. Freeze source evidence at reservation/issue or explicitly invalidate dependent execution when that evidence changes. Physical stock quantities and identities cannot be created by planning or recovered by merely deleting an allocation record.

Actual execution must dispatch through a real company-owned internal origin, with no synthetic SO/account or writes to customer manufacturingAllocated/fulfillmentTallies. Add operations, process loss, exact input provenance, actual output identities, output QC partition and a separate authorized company-stock output receipt. Issuing a manufactured assembly later must not consume its original raw materials again. Valuation remains UNVALUED until a separately accepted accounting authority exists; this local stock increment does not establish financial posting or provider integration.

Manufactured-child custody, typed graph edges, shared graph write/cycle bounds, full descendant evidence freeze and authorized spare/rework replacement remain later gates. Keep original canonical E01 fixtures unchanged: no invented incoming serials, retrospective catalog adoption or altered historical demand to make a supplemental journey pass. Existing first 100 item/vendor source-picker navigation is still a named UI continuation from MAP-06. Local tests, a local commit, push, merge, deployment and live integration are separate outcomes.
