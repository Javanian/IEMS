# Independent received-installation probe

`received-installation.test.mjs` uses actual authenticated HTTP on the marked owned replica helper. Its supplemental fixture has four parent outputs in two WOs and eight explicitly adopted/claimed/consumed incoming identities at ratio2. It neither imports provider test bodies nor invents incoming serials for canonical E01.

The main WO covers corrupt registry origin, outbox rollback including graph/head/source versions and durable key, simultaneous HTTP competition between two parents for one child, overratio refusal, historical recording under a later hold, exact completion, K1 correction invalidation and explicit re-finalization, coordinated K1/installation shipment freezing, later hold/replay behavior, immutable pointers and committing replay revocation. The separate WO exercises permanent removal, same/different-parent reuse and loss refusal, and deliberately remains incomplete because no replacement/rework policy exists. Every source remains CONSUMED and original stock counters/MATERIAL_CONSUMED records are conserved.

The two-parent calls are concurrent HTTP requests, without an internal two-writer barrier. The hold/revocation interventions occur after middleware authorization but before the transaction guard; they are deterministic command orderings, not two-snapshot proof. Existing shared tenant/plant authorization writes serialize same-plant guarded transactions. Source review of the explicit graph/head/WO/source-unit fences and unique child projection is additional evidence; this test alone does not isolate the graph lock from authorization locks.

Registry-origin and head-authority corruption are direct owned-fixture injections restored in finally; the outbox validator is also restored in finally. Head probes check status/revision disagreement, invalid version and malformed freeze metadata on both K1 and installation authority. The exact false-completion case uses a real balanced K1 DRAFT correction, changes only its head status to COMPLETE, and requires both installation read and fresh finalization to fail without domain effects. Full business snapshots exclude entire sessions/memberships/users/tenants/plants/legalEntities/credentials collections and the owned marker. They do not certify unrelated authorization-record immutability.

Received identities are leaves by contract, so reciprocal manufactured cycles are inapplicable here and are not counted as passed. This probe does not close manufactured-child custody/ownership, general graph cycles, replacement, explicit positive process-loss/zero-good installation, every scope/revocation case, maximum-size load, POD or full E01. Provider and lead evidence must cover remaining bounded received-only acceptance.
