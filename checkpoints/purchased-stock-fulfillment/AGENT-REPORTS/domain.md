## Implemented boundary

The existing catalog stock-use head, reservation revisions, receipt version and actual serial occupancy now cover both internal-production demand and explicit purchased sales-order demand. Opening QC/putaway balances, opening units and received-origin evidence remain immutable. Customer dispatch reduces the shared on-hand/available projection through catalogCustomerShipments; it creates neither a warehouse SHIP projection nor manufactured consumption.

A mixed catalog sales order can fulfill its proven PURCHASED lines through the new route. MANUFACTURED lines remain unsupported. A customer reservation may ship partially and then release all remaining unshipped stock even under a later hold or dependency withdrawal; actual shipped quantities and unit identities stay terminal. A separate sales-order demand head enforces cross-receipt line capacity without mutating the frozen sales-order authority.

POD, invoice, payment and document preparation validate actual purchased shipment evidence. Full initial invoice snapshots and actual payment before/after invoice revisions bind settlement fields. A separate acyclic billing aggregate validates physical shipment references, invoice/payment/key sets and both shipment and sales-order invoice tallies. The existing balances, entitlement and reconciliation reads validate the typed authorities in one snapshot. Financial PDFs retain the existing financial/delivery rendering contract and are not catalog certificates.

Fresh reserve/dispatch requires current item, location/bin, strict hold and owner authority, with existing authorization and real stock/dependency fences. Historical post-dispatch POD/billing and exact command replay do not require a withdrawn source to become freshly eligible. Replay still checks current action authorization and historical proof before return. Reserve/release replay returns an immutable command receipt beside the current validated view; it does not promise a historical full-response snapshot.

## Limits

This is a purchased-stock adapter, with no manufactured-stock dispatch, inferred item matching, legacy receipt adoption, return/amendment or cross-plant flow. Typed payments may settle invoices of one typed sales order only; mixed legacy/typed or multi-order settlement is refused. Bounds are100 claims per source/SO,400 source/consumer events,100 shipments and100 typed invoices/payments per SO,20 sources per shipment and100 selected serial identities. History-cap readiness is fail-closed; a successful maximum-size capacity campaign has not been performed. F5 remains BLOCKED and its authorization fences were not relaxed.
